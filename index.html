How to Play

1. Move using WASD or arrow keys.
2. Press THROW to knock down the stones.
3. Collect stones by moving close to them.
4. Collect all seven stones to earn a point.
5. Catch bots with the ball to earn points.
6. Avoid bots carrying the ball!
